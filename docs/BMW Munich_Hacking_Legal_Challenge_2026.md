🚀Munich Hacking Legal Challenge 
🚀 Building the AI Legal Agent Pla1orm for BMW Group The central ques.on How can AI agents support the legal department of a global corporation in such a way that lawyers work hand-in-hand with AI agents in strategic, legal, value-adding work? The Challenge Develop an AI agent platform for the legal department – an intelligent, collaborative multi-agent environment that accepts, processes, delegates, reconciles legal tasks autonomously and returns the results to humans in a structured way. Imagine a solution like Moltbook 1 – but specialized in the legal and international requirements of a corporate legal department. The Vision The goal is to build a scalable, modular AI agent platform that: • receives, recognises and classifies legal tasks • is specialized on AI agents • coordinates tasks and delegates them between agents • consolidates results in a quality-assured manner • ensures transparency, traceability and governance • and interact seamlessly with human lawyers Possible Use Cases Typical tasks of a corporate legal department include, but are not limited to, the following, which could be translated into agent logic, such as: • 
📜 Contract drafting, review and negotiation • 
🌍 Country and jurisdiction-specific legal searches (esp. DE, USA, UK, FR) • 
⚖ Processing of dispute cases (in and out of court) • 
📨 Answering internal legal inquiries (e.g. from specialist agencies) • 
🔎 Compliance checks (e.g. sanctions lists, supply chain law, ESG) • 
📚 Knowledge Management & Precedent Search • 
🏡 Administrative and non-legal activities (e.g. appointment coordination, auditing)   1 Moltbook is an AI-na/ve work pla5orm in which several specialized AI agents collabora/vely take on, work on, and coordinate tasks with each other. Similar to a digital team of experts, agents can accept work packages, share results, coordinate intermediate steps, and return structured results to humans. The focus is on orchestra/on, role logic, and scalable collabora/on between autonomous AI instances within a shared workspace.
